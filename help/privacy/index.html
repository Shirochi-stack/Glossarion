Google Drive privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026. This policy covers the optional Google Drive features of Glossarion on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS: Google Drive sync and sharing a book through Drive. Glossarion works without them, and nothing is sent to Google until you sign in and turn one of them on.

Permission and use

Google handles the sign-in and asks for your consent. Glossarion requests one permission, drive.file. With it Glossarion can create files and folders in your Google Drive and manage the ones it created. It cannot see or change anything else in your Drive.

Glossarion uses this permission only to do what you ask: upload your books to a Drive folder (Google Drive sync) and create a share link for a file (Share via link).

Data sent to Google

When a sync or a share runs, Glossarion sends the files you chose for it, their file names and the name of the Drive folder, directly from your device to Google's Drive API over HTTPS. Depending on the setting you picked, the files are a book's compiled EPUB or PDF, its whole output folder, or the output folder together with the raw source file.

When you share a file through Drive, Glossarion also sets that file to "anyone with the link can view" so the link works. You decide which books are uploaded and who gets a link.

Google stores the files in the account you signed in with. Glossarion has no server of its own: its developers receive none of your files, your Google account details or your sign-in.

What is stored on your device

  • Windows, macOS and Linux: the Google sign-in (a refresh token) is stored encrypted in library_cloud.json next to Glossarion's configuration file, so later uploads do not need a new sign-in.
  • iOS: the refresh token is stored encrypted, with the key kept in the system Keychain.
  • Android: Google Play services keeps the authorization; Glossarion keeps a note that Drive is connected.

Access tokens are kept in memory only. Share links and the time of the last sync are kept on your device.

Retention and control

Sign out (desktop: Library settings; mobile: Settings › Cloud sync & sharing) removes the saved sign-in from your device. On Windows, macOS, Linux and iOS it also asks Google to revoke Glossarion's access. On Android the authorization is kept by Google Play services until you remove it.

On every platform you can remove Glossarion's access yourself in your Google Account under third-party connections.

Files that were uploaded stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there. To change who can open a shared file, change its sharing in Drive.

Google API data policy

Glossarion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's own handling of your account and files is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about how Glossarion handles Google Drive data: open an issue on GitHub or ask on Discord.
